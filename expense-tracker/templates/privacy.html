Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects information you provide directly to us when you create an account and use our service. This includes:

  • Account information: name, email address, and password
  • Financial data: expense records, amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions
  • Device information: browser type, IP address, and pages visited
  • Usage data: how you interact with Spendly features and functionality

We do not collect payment information — Spendly is currently free to use.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our service
  • Process and fulfill your requests
  • Send you technical notices and support messages
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and requests
  • Improve and develop new features for Spendly
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities for security and system administration

We will never sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers using industry-standard encryption and security measures. We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and destruction.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You use Spendly at your own risk.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials. You are responsible for all activities that occur under your account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may contain links to third-party websites and services that are not operated by us. This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party services, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services before providing your information.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information and expense data for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications from us

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy on our website and updating the "Last updated" date above.

Your continued use of Spendly following the posting of revised Privacy Policy means you accept and agree to the changes.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at support@spendly.app. We will respond to your inquiry within 30 days.
